Original PNG frames are bundled unchanged. Type Strike uses wizzard_m idle frames,
tiny_slug/slug animation frames, dungeon floor/wall tiles, columns and banners.
Runtime adaptations: nearest-neighbor scaling, palette variants for enemy kinds,
armor/crystal/fuse/healer markers and a dark overlay on tiled backgrounds.
No extra collision walls or gameplay obstacles are introduced by the artwork.
